Paul Anson

Active Angel Investor · Board Chair · Scale-up Operator

Nick Balon

Exited Founder · CEO · Angel Investor

Nick built and exited CRFS, growing it into a defence technology company sold to private equity and then Motorola Solutions. Previously COO at Xennia, he helped propel the industrial inkjet company to market leadership. Nick is an active angel investor and mentor, with degrees from Oxford and Yale.

Jamie Bignal

Director at HSBC Innovation Banking, supporting technology and innovation businesses from Cambridge.

Hugh Campbell

Founder, GP Bullhound

Hugh is a Co-founder and Managing Partner of GP Bullhound, a premier global technology advisory and investment firm. Since co-founding the firm after a stint at Citi and Goldman Sachs, he has become a leading figure in the European tech ecosystem. Based in the UK, he was the driving force behind the opening of GP Bullhound's Manchester office in 2013 and is a fierce advocate for the Northern Powerhouse.

Siobhan Clarke

David Cleevely

Richard Fallon

Richard Faulkner

Head of Regional Coverage · HSBC Innovation Banking

Pam Garside

A former Chair of Cambridge Angels and Fellow of Cambridge Judge, she co-chairs The Cambridge Health Network and advises accelerators, VCs and start-ups.

Phil Gold

Co-founder at Zapt.ai

An experienced business executive and investor with a track record of successful ventures encompassing consultancy, built environment and technology. Currently co-founder of ZAPT (one of GM's Rising Stars of Business 2025), a platform that gets SMEs up and running immediately with custom software tailored exactly to the way they work, at a fraction of the cost of more traditional solutions.

Poppy Gustafsson

Tech Executive · Former Darktrace CEO · Life Peer

Poppy Gustafsson is a British tech executive and life peer who served as UK Minister for Investment. She co-founded and led cybersecurity firm Darktrace, which uses self-learning AI to combat cyber threats. A mathematician and chartered accountant, her career includes Amadeus Capital and HP Autonomy.

Cat Headley

CEO, University of Manchester Innovation Factory

Chief Executive Officer of the University of Manchester Innovation Factory. Catherine brings extensive experience working at the interface between academia and the commercial world, building partnerships and programmes that support knowledge exchange, collaboration and the commercialisation of research.

Faye Holland

Partnership Director · Cambridge × Manchester Partnership

Faye Holland is Partnership Director for The Cambridge x Manchester Partnership - the UK's first cross-city innovation initiative connecting two of the country's leading clusters. She leads strategy delivery, stakeholder alignment, and performance across the programme, with a focus on developing new opportunities, attracting global investment, and delivering good growth through innovation. Faye's career spans over three decades in global tech - from PIPEX and IBM through to founding an innovation communications consultancy acquired in 2025. She is Chair of the Cambridgeshire Chamber of Commerce, Entrepreneur in Residence at Anglia Ruskin University, and co-hosts the Cambridge Tech Podcast. Regularly named among the UK's most influential women in tech, Faye is known for connecting research, capital, and enterprise - and for telling the stories of the companies doing it best.

David Levine

Principal

He is CEO of Glenluna Ventures and founder and ex-CEO of DigitalBridge (now Fixtuur), which used AI and computer vision to help people design their bathrooms and kitchens.
